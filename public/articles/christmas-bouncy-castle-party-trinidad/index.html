Give the children their own Christmas activity

Family gatherings and Christmas parties can bring together children of different ages. A bouncy castle gives them a dedicated place to play while adults organise food, music and time together. It can be a useful centrepiece for a family lime, neighbourhood celebration or children's Christmas party.

Meet the Spider-Man Combo Bouncer

The Spider-Man Combo Bouncer combines several activities in one inflatable unit:

  • A bouncing area for active play.
  • A climbing wall for an extra challenge.
  • A slide that can be set up for wet or dry use.

The unit is designed for up to 8 children at a time. Adult supervision is required throughout play, and children should follow the safety instructions given at setup.

Make sure your yard has enough room

The bouncer measures approximately 26 ft long × 13 ft wide × 13 ft high. Measure the intended setup area before enquiring and allow additional clear space around the unit. Check for low branches, overhead wires, uneven ground and anything that could interfere with safe installation.

Plan the setup before guests arrive

  • Choose a flat, suitable area with enough clearance.
  • Make sure a suitable electrical outlet is available for the blower.
  • Keep the play area clear of tables, decorations and vehicle movement.
  • Decide whether you want the slide used wet or dry and mention this when enquiring.
  • Arrange adult supervision for the full period of use.

Book ahead for the Christmas period

Christmas dates can be popular for family events. Send your preferred date, location and rental duration so availability and setup arrangements can be checked. No deposit is required; payment is due on delivery.
