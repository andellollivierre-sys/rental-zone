Check that the generator suits your equipment

Do not choose a generator based only on the number of appliances. Add the running power requirements of equipment used together and check whether any item needs extra starting power. Motors, pumps and compressors can draw more power when starting. If you are unsure, send the equipment list to the supplier so suitability can be checked before you confirm.

Check the site and safety requirements

A generator must operate outdoors in a well-ventilated location, away from doors, windows and air intakes. Never run it inside a building, garage, enclosed tent or other enclosed space. Exhaust contains carbon monoxide, a poisonous gas. Keep the generator dry while allowing proper ventilation, and use correctly rated cables and connections.
