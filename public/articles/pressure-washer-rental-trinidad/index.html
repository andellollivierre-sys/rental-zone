What can a pressure washer help you clean?

A pressure washer can make some outdoor cleaning tasks quicker by directing water at a surface under pressure. Depending on the machine and the surface, common jobs may include:

  • Concrete driveways and walkways.
  • Outdoor patios and paved areas.
  • Suitable exterior walls and hard surfaces.
  • Outdoor equipment and other washable surfaces.

Always check that the surface and material can safely withstand pressure washing. High pressure can damage paint, wood, soft stone, seals and other delicate finishes.

Prepare before you start

A little preparation helps you work safely and avoid unnecessary damage.

  • Move vehicles, furniture and loose objects away from the cleaning area.
  • Keep children and pets clear of the work zone.
  • Protect electrical outlets, fittings and other items that should not get wet.
  • Clear loose debris before washing.
  • Test the spray on a small, inconspicuous area first.

Use the right technique

Start with a wider spray pattern and keep the nozzle moving. Avoid holding a concentrated jet close to one spot. Increase pressure or move closer only when the surface can safely handle it. Never point the spray at people, animals, electrical equipment or yourself.
