Meet the Spider-Man Combo Bouncer

This combo unit brings several activities together in one inflatable attraction. Children can enjoy the bouncing area, climb the wall and finish with a slide. It can be used with a wet or dry slide, depending on the setup and conditions.

  • Bouncing area for active play.
  • Climbing wall for an extra challenge.
  • Slide that can be used wet or dry.
  • Designed for up to 8 children at a time.

Check your space before booking

The Spider-Man Combo Bouncer measures approximately 26 ft long × 13 ft wide × 13 ft high. Before arranging your rental, measure the setup area and allow clear space around the unit. The surface should be flat and suitable for installation, with enough overhead clearance from branches, wires and other obstacles.

What do you need on setup day?

The blower needs a continuous power supply. A standard household electrical outlet should be available within approximately 100 feet of the setup area. If power is not close enough, discuss a suitable alternative with us before the event.

Wet or dry slide?

The slide can be arranged for dry use or wet use. If you want the wet setup, let us know when enquiring so the arrangement can be discussed in advance. Adult supervision is important throughout play, and children should follow the safety instructions provided at setup.

How to arrange your rental

Send us your event date, location and preferred rental duration. We can then check availability and confirm the arrangements for your event.
