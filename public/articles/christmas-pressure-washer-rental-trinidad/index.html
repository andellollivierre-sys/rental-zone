Start with the outdoor areas guests will notice

Driveways, walkways, patios and paved entertaining areas can collect dirt, algae and grime over time. Cleaning these areas before decorating and welcoming visitors can make the property feel more prepared for the season.

A pressure washer directs water at a surface under pressure. It can help with suitable outdoor cleaning jobs, but the correct technique and pressure depend on the surface and the machine.

What can you clean?

  • Concrete driveways and walkways.
  • Suitable outdoor tiles and paved patios.
  • Some exterior walls and hard surfaces, where pressure washing is appropriate.
  • Outdoor areas around bins and utility spaces.

Do not assume every surface is suitable. High pressure can damage paint, wood, soft stone, grout, seals and delicate finishes. Check first and test a small, inconspicuous area.

Work through your Christmas cleaning in stages

  • Move vehicles, furniture, planters and loose decorations out of the work area.
  • Clear leaves and loose debris before washing.
  • Clean the driveway and walkways first, then move to suitable patio areas.
  • Allow surfaces to dry before putting furniture or decorations back.
  • Keep children and pets away from the cleaning area until the work is finished.

Use the machine safely

Wear suitable footwear and eye protection. Keep the nozzle moving, start with a wider spray pattern and avoid directing a concentrated jet at one spot. Never point the spray at people, animals, electrical equipment or yourself. Protect electrical outlets and fittings from water.
